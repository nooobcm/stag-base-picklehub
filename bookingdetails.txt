## 2. Customer booking flow

The customer booking process should remain:

1. Select the court, date, and time.
2. Select a payment method.
3. Click Book Now.
4. The booking is submitted for admin verification.

There are two payment methods:

### A. Cash

- Customers can book without uploading payment proof.
- Cash bookings do not require a deposit.
- The booking should be submitted for admin verification.
- Once the admin approves the booking, its payment status should be **Unpaid** if no payment has been collected.
- In Admin Booking Details, display only the **Balance Payment** section.
- Do not display an empty Deposit section for cash bookings.

The customer can pay the full amount or make partial payments when they arrive at the court.

### B. GCash / InstaPay

- Customers must upload their payment proof before submitting the booking.
- The booking remains pending admin verification.
- The admin must be able to view the uploaded proof before approving the booking.
- The admin determines how much was actually received based on the submitted proof.

There are two possible outcomes:

**If the customer paid in full:**
- The admin verifies the proof.
- The admin records the amount received.
- The amount received equals the total booking amount.
- No additional payment method selection is necessary for this initial payment because the customer already selected GCash/InstaPay.
- The booking can be approved.
- Payment status becomes **Fully Paid**.
- No outstanding balance remains.

**If the customer paid only a deposit or partial amount:**
- The admin verifies the proof.
- The admin enters the actual amount received.
- The system calculates the remaining balance automatically.
- The admin can approve the booking even though it is not fully paid.
- The booking becomes confirmed/booked.
- Payment status becomes **Partially Paid**.
- The customer must pay the remaining balance when they arrive at the court.

Important: A partially paid booking must NOT remain pending simply because the customer has not paid the full amount. Once the admin verifies the proof and approves the booking, it must become a confirmed booking with a Partially Paid status.

## 4. Automatic payment calculations

The system must calculate payment totals automatically.

Use these rules:

**Total Amount**
= Total booking fee.

**Total Amount Received**
= Sum of all successfully recorded payments.

**Outstanding Balance**
= Total Amount - Total Amount Received.

**Payment Status**
- Unpaid: No payment has been received.
- Partially Paid: Some payment has been received, but the outstanding balance is greater than zero.
- Fully Paid: The total amount received equals the total booking amount.

Example:

Total booking amount: ₱1,000

Initial GCash deposit: ₱500

Outstanding balance: ₱500

The admin records another payment of ₱300.

Total received: ₱800

Outstanding balance: ₱200

Payment status: Partially Paid

The customer later pays the remaining ₱200.

Total received: ₱1,000

Outstanding balance: ₱0

Payment status: Fully Paid

These calculations must be based on actual payment records, not manually editable payment status labels.

Prevent the system from accepting payments that exceed the outstanding balance unless the existing business rules explicitly support overpayments. Validate all amounts on the server/database side, not only in the frontend.

Use proper decimal/numeric handling for Philippine peso amounts. Avoid floating-point calculation errors.

## 5. Recording additional balance payments

When a customer arrives at the court, the admin should be able to record another payment from the Booking Details page.

The process should be:

1. Open the confirmed booking.
2. View the outstanding balance.
3. Enter the amount received.
4. Select the payment method (Cash or GCash).
5. Select or automatically identify the staff member who received the payment.
6. Save payment details.
7. Update the total amount received and outstanding balance automatically.
8. Update the payment status to Partially Paid or Fully Paid.

The admin must be able to record multiple additional payments until the outstanding balance reaches zero.

Do not overwrite previous payments. Every payment must be stored as a separate transaction record so the system maintains a complete payment history.

## 6. Payment history and auditability

Every recorded payment should preserve:

- Payment amount
- Payment method
- Payment type (initial deposit or balance payment)
- Payment date and time
- Staff/admin who received or verified the payment
- Related booking ID
- Payment proof reference, when applicable
- Verification/recording status

The admin should be able to review the payment history for a booking.

Previous payment records must not disappear when another payment is added.

Avoid allowing an admin to accidentally record the same payment twice. Use appropriate validation and database constraints where necessary.

## 7. Important booking and payment rules

Keep booking status separate from payment status.

Examples:

| Booking status | Payment status |
|---|---|
| Pending verification | Unpaid or pending verification |
| Confirmed | Unpaid |
| Confirmed | Partially Paid |
| Confirmed | Fully Paid |
| Cancelled | Preserve payment history and handle refunds according to existing rules |

Do not automatically cancel a confirmed booking simply because it has an outstanding balance.

Do not mark a booking as fully paid just because the admin approves it. It must only become Fully Paid when the sum of recorded payments equals the total booking amount.

For a GCash booking, approval of the proof and confirmation of the booking must be handled correctly even when the verified payment is only partial.

For a Cash booking, the admin must be able to approve the booking without requiring a deposit or payment proof.
